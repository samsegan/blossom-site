      Drum & Blossom

      About

      Blossom is a London-born, Phoenix-raised producer, songwriter, vocalist and DJ.

      She trained as a violist for 12 years before she found dance music, took production courses at Dubspot in Los Angeles in 2015, and was named Best DJ in Phoenix by the Phoenix New Times.

      She came up through bass house on Night Bass and IN / ROTATION, then turned to drum & bass on her Twitch streams in 2020. Since then: Surreal, About You, Stay and Stronger on UKF, Ride 4 U on Deadbeats, Steamroller with Inja on Drum&BassArena, a run of singles on Helix, Good Vibrations on Monstercat and Loosen Up on Bassrush Records.

      She has played EDC Las Vegas, Shambhala, HARD Summer, Liquicity, Lost Lands and Coachella, recorded a DNB60 mix for Charlie Tee on BBC Radio 1, and in 2026 supported Subtronics across North America and toured Australia with ShockOne.
